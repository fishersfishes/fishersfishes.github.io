About Us

Welcome to Fisher's Fishes, a global, virtual, student-led community of budding quantitative biologists.

In the absence of a formal curriculum and guidance on how to start in this interdisciplinary field, we help each other learn by hosting a curated mix of talks by faculty and peers. We simultaneously function as a platform where people can present things that spark their curiosity, whether that's a paper they wrote, their master’s thesis project, or just a paper they're interested in and want to dig into with others.

Our primarily online format lets us put a diverse set of people in the same (zoom) room without significant resources. Over about two and a half years we've held around 30 talks on a wide range of topics, from ecology and evolution to biophysics, mathematical oncology, and even the history of science.

We are passionate about expanding access to quantitative biology in the Global South, where the support for this exciting, interdisciplinary field is thinnest. A lot of people never find out that quantitative biology is a path open to them at all, and that lack of awareness is something we can take on directly. Anyone who wants to foster a community like this in their own university or region is welcome to join us, and as more and more people connect we shall form a network offering many invaluable perspectives.

Fisher's Fishes began as a small theoretical biology journal club at IISER Pune. As students and colleagues from other places joined, it grew into a virtual society that now connects members across institutes around the world. We bring together people working in eco evolutionary dynamics, biophysics, systems and synthetic biology, population genetics, neuroscience, mathematical oncology, and many emerging fields that use theory to understand complex biological phenomena. Our community includes undergraduates, graduate students, and postdoctoral scholars, and many of our events are led by students who present papers they find interesting or talk about their own work.

If this initiative excites you, please reach out to us! You can email us at fishesfishers@gmail.com or join Google Groups, YouTube, and Bluesky.
